Engineering tools.

Calculators we built for our own design work: they check members to NDS, AISC and ASCE 7 and produce Tedds-style calculation reports you can print to PDF.

For qualified users. These tools support preliminary design and checking. Results depend on the inputs and assumptions you enter and must be reviewed by a licensed engineer before they are used for construction or permit submittal.
