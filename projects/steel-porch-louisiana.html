Steel Porch with Standing-Seam Metal Roof

A new galvanized-steel porch attached to an existing house, with 4×4 steel posts and beams, C-channel rafters and a standing-seam metal roof.

Scope of work

  • Porch foundation plan, layout plan and framing plan
  • 4×4×11 ga galvanized steel posts and beams; 2×4×14 ga C-channel rafters
  • Post-to-base-plate, beam-to-column and rafter-to-beam connections
  • Roof-to-existing-wall ledger and receiver details; 26 ga standing-seam roof system

St. George, LA
