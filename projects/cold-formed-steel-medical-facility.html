Cold-Formed Steel Ceiling & Wall Framing

Cold-formed steel wall and ceiling framing for a medical-facility interior, with C8×18.75 channel beams, CFS ceiling joists, slab-on-ground foundations and connection details.

Scope of work

  • Foundation plan with 4″ slab on ground and ½″Ø adhesive anchors @ 24″ O.C.
  • Wall layout and elevations for 362S162-68 cold-formed steel stud walls @ 16″ O.C.
  • Ceiling structural plans with C8×18.75 channels (2 rows #10 screws @ 16″) and 800S200-68 CFS joists
  • Cold-formed steel details: tracks, bridging, clips and strapping against beam rotation
  • Calculations for steel beams (AISC 360), CFS joists and studs (AISI S100 / S240) and footings (ACI 318)
