Container Coffee Stand — CFS Walls & Footings

Calculations for a container-based coffee stand: cold-formed steel wall studs for each elevation and the strip footings beneath them.

Scope of work

  • Front, rear and side walls in 600S162-97 cold-formed steel studs @ 16″ O.C. (12′ walls)
  • Combined axial and lateral (35 psf) stud checks to AISI S100 (ASD)
  • Strip footing analysis and design for each wall line (ACI 318)
