RCC Framed Structure — Columns, Beams, Slab & Footings

Analysis, design and reinforcement detailing of a single-storey reinforced-concrete frame to IS 456: ten columns on isolated footings, beams spanning up to 9.1 m and a one-way slab.

Scope of work

  • STAAD.Pro frame model with limit-state load combinations and concrete design to IS 456
  • Column, beam, slab-panel and footing layout plans
  • 600 × 450 columns with 4-T25 + 8-T20 bars and T8 links — M25 concrete, Fe500 steel, 50 mm cover
  • 300 × 600 beams for the 9.1 m (30 ft) span and 300 × 450 beams for the shorter spans
  • 150 mm one-way slab with alternate bars bent up
  • 2400 × 2400 isolated footings on 150 mm PCC, with plan, sections and reinforcement
