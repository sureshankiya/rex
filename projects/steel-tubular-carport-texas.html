40′ × 80′ Steel Tubular Carport Building

Long-span tubular-steel carport / utility building designed for 145 mph wind, with laced columns, trusses, purlins and slab foundation.

Scope of work

  • Design loads: 145 mph ultimate wind, Exposure C, Risk Category II, 5 psf ground snow
  • Foundation, column, truss and purlin layout plans for an 80′ length
  • Ladder-leg / laced tubular columns and trusses with full member legend
  • Truss-to-post and roof-rafter connection details
