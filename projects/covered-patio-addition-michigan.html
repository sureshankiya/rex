Covered Patio Addition with LVL Beams

A new patio roof tied to an existing house, framed with 6×6 posts, triple LVL beams and an 8:12 engineered-wood roof.

  • (3) 1¾" × 16" LVL beams on 6×6 posts with Simpson post bases and caps
  • (2) 2×10 ridge beam and engineered-wood rafters at an 8:12 slope
  • House / roof interface and typical connection details
