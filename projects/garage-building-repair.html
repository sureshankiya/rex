Six-Bay Garage Building Repair

A repair scheme for a two-level, six-bay garage building: damaged wall sections and framing are removed and replaced, with new floor joists and strapping.

Scope of work

  • Wall layout plan identifying damaged wall sections and framing to be replaced
  • Wall elevation details with 1-hr rated assemblies (UL U465)
  • New 2×10 floor joists @ 16" O.C. and roof framing at 2×10 @ 24" O.C.
  • Simpson MST / coil strapping and shear-transfer details
