Podium Steel Wall Cover — Analysis & Connection Design

Analysis model and connection design for a steel chequered-plate wall cover on a high-rise podium, including a consultant-comment response and an IDEA StatiCa connection check.

Scope of work

  • STAAD.Pro geometry model of the channel / angle frame (CH-1 150×75 channel, AE-1 60×60×5 angle, 4 mm plate)
  • Section assignment, supports and load application; deflection and member utilisation checks
  • Fin-plate and base-plate connection design to AISC 360 (LRFD)
  • CBFEM angle-to-channel connection check in IDEA StatiCa
  • Load traceability from analysis reactions to connection loads; response to consultant comments
