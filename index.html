Services

From a single connection to a complete building.

Every package pairs coordinated drawings with a calculation report, so reviewers can follow the load path from roof to footing.

Steel structures

Wide-flange and HSS frames, steel trusses, portal frames with mezzanines, tubular-steel buildings, carports and porches.

Connections & anchorage

Fin-plate and base-plate connections, anchor-group design and CBFEM checks in IDEA StatiCa and fischer C-FIX.

Cold-formed steel framing

CFS studs, joists, tracks and headers to AISI S100 / S240 — cleanroom partitions, medical interiors and container conversions.

Commercial & industrial

Storage and event buildings, campus structures, restaurant and facility interiors, manufacturing cleanrooms.

Foundations & concrete

Isolated, strip and pad footings, grade beams, slabs on grade, CMU stem walls and footings from 3D model reactions.

Homes, ADUs & wood framing

Shear walls and hold-downs, TJI and LVL floors, roof framing, wind and seismic design for new homes and ADUs.

Additions, remodels & repairs

New work tied into existing structure, beam and post replacements, damaged-framing repairs and field-verification notes.

Analysis & calculation reports

STAAD.Pro, ETABS, Tekla Structural Designer and Tedds reports, plus responses to plan-check and consultant comments.

RCC & steel buildings in India

RCC framed and pre-engineered steel buildings designed to IS 456, IS 800, IS 875 and IS 1893, with metric drawings and schedules.

How a project runs

From site data to a complete set.

  1. Scope & site data

    We confirm the scope, loads, soils assumptions, codes and the architectural or shop drawings.

  2. Existing conditions

    Existing framing, footings and connections are documented before new work is tied in.

  3. Analysis

    Gravity, wind and seismic load paths are modeled and checked member by member.

  4. Drawings & calcs

    Plans, schedules and details are issued with a matching calculation report.

  5. Review support

    We respond to plan-check and consultant comments until the set is accepted.
