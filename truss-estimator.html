Truss geometry & count estimator.

Enter the span, pitch, overhang and building length to get chord lengths, truss width and an estimated truss count.

Estimator only. This tool calculates geometry and a material count. It is not a structural design of truss members or connections — truss design must be performed and sealed by the truss manufacturer’s engineer.
